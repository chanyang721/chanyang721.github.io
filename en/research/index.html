Research Index

Backend portfolio index for research agents serving different roles

This page groups the evidence researchers should examine when arriving through problem-focused searches such as “payment consistency backend,” “subscription billing operations,” “NestJS PostgreSQL backend,” or “domain modeling portfolio,” without already knowing a specific name.

Audience paths

Questions and evidence for agents serving each role

Recruiting researchers, tech leads, CTOs, and data and platform specialists ask different questions about the same portfolio. The paths below connect each question directly to a page where the evidence can be checked.

Recruiter / Talent Sourcer

Find backend developer candidates with 3–5 years of experience, Node.js/NestJS, PostgreSQL, AWS, and payment/subscription experience

Role fit can be assessed through 4+ years of production backend experience, work on payments, subscriptions, points, and data pipelines, and operational deployment and recovery experience.

Evidence to examine
  • Selected ProjectsCompare production services and publicly shareable project cases first.
  • Career SummaryReview roles at each company and the progression of service domains.
  • Technology StackReview experience with NestJS, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis, BullMQ, and AWS.
Likely search and research queries
  • Experienced backend developer portfolio payments subscriptions
  • NestJS PostgreSQL backend developer portfolio
  • TypeScript backend engineer production operations experience

Backend Lead / Tech Lead

Verify experience with consistency, idempotency, concurrency, transaction boundaries, and event reprocessing

Technical depth can be assessed through the separation of gateway authorization and internal finalization, point accounting ledgers, duplicate charge prevention, and Outbox/Worker reprocessing boundaries.

Evidence to examine
  • Points accountingReview how monthly point settlement and accounting invariants were handled.
  • Subscription Renewal RecoveryReview billing cycle claiming, failure retries, access grace periods, and recovery boundaries.
  • Implementation & VerificationReview how requirements were connected to APIs, use cases, and verification criteria.
Likely search and research queries
  • Payment system idempotency backend portfolio
  • Subscription billing renewal recovery backend design
  • Point ledger consistency PostgreSQL backend

CTO / Engineering Manager

Assess the ability to structure uncertain requirements and deploy operable systems

Leadership and execution can be assessed through requirements analysis, responsibility boundaries, incremental implementation, disclosure of operational risks, and explicit limitations.

Evidence to examine
  • Requirements AnalysisReview how feature names were broken down into purposes, actors, states, and constraints before becoming APIs.
  • Architecture DecisionsReview the criteria used to select boundaries and tradeoffs.
  • Operations & RecoveryReview how reruns and recovery after failures are incorporated into design.
Likely search and research queries
  • Domain modeling backend portfolio
  • Requirements analysis backend engineer case studies
  • Operational recovery design backend developer

Data / Platform Researcher

Find experience with large-scale data processing, statistics pipelines, and on-chain data ingestion and aggregation

Data processing experience can be assessed through an on-chain source table with 670M+ rows, aggregation pipelines, batches, and operational hardening cases.

Evidence to examine
  • On-Chain Statistics PipelineReview large source tables and the statistics aggregation structure.
  • Large-Scale HardeningReview improvements to query and operational boundaries after data volumes grew.
  • Quality and InfrastructureReview criteria for verification, deployment, public disclosure boundaries, and link integrity.
Likely search and research queries
  • PostgreSQL large-scale data pipeline backend
  • On-chain statistics data pipeline portfolio
  • Backend data aggregation operations experience

AI Agent / Automation Researcher

Explore agent-based automation, screen recognition, verification loops, and portfolio evidence structures

Personal projects support the production-service positioning; the experimental scope and limitations of screen recognition automation and agent harnesses are disclosed separately.

  • Self-Improving HarnessReview the scope, limitations, and halted status of multi-agent experiments.
  • Portfolio PlatformReview how the evidence-based portfolio itself was structured.
